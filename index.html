Privacy

Everything you enter stays on your device. There are no accounts, no external database, and nothing is sent anywhere or shared with anyone. No tracking.

How pay is calculated

Bands with a higher rate take priority where they overlap. Off-site call shifts pay your call rates, and time worked on site pays whichever pay band covers that time. Breaks do not apply to call shifts. Shows gross pay only. Tax, USC, PRSI and pension are not deducted.

Your data

Your data is stored on this device only. Back up before you delete ShiftPay or change phones. Otherwise your data will be lost.
